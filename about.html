About Solitaire Finz Mart

Guiding borrowers through better lending decisions

SOLITAIRE FINZ MART is a loan and financial advisory practice based in Bhiwandi, Thane, helping individuals and businesses navigate home loans, business finance, property finance and balance transfers.

Our Story

Fifteen-plus years, one file at a time

Our Mission

To provide transparent, responsible guidance that helps customers understand their loan options clearly — simplifying a process that is often confusing, and putting the customer's interest first at every step.

Our Vision

To be a dependable, long-term financial partner for individuals and businesses across the region — known for clarity, follow-through and honest advice, not just transaction volume.

Our Values

  • Integrity in every recommendation
  • Transparency on costs and timelines
  • Customer-first decision making
  • Professionalism and accountability
  • Long-term relationships over one-time transactions
